## 1. Executive Summary & Business Context

**Olist** is the largest e-commerce department store marketplace enabler in Brazil. It connects small merchants across Brazil directly to main marketplaces with a single contract. Sellers list their products on Olist, and Olist coordinates order fulfillment, logistics partners, and automated post-delivery customer satisfaction surveys.

In modern e-commerce, customer acquisition costs (CAC) continue to escalate. Relying solely on acquiring new buyers is unsustainable. To drive sustainable profitability, Olist must optimize **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** and **Retention** through data-driven **Customer Segmentation**.

This report presents a complete end-to-end Machine Learning pipeline that transforms raw transactional data across 9 relational tables into 4 distinct, actionable customer personas.

## 3. Data Architecture & Multi-Level Merging Strategy

### 3.1 Key Schema Pitfall: `customer_id` vs `customer_unique_id`
- `customer_id`: Key assigned per transaction/order. Aggregating by this key erroneously implies every customer buys exactly once.
- `customer_unique_id`: Unique identifier for an actual individual person. **Customer segmentation must aggregate features at the `customer_unique_id` level.**

### 3.2 Relational Data Merging Pipeline
```text
[order_items] + [products] + [translation] ──► Order Items Aggregated (Price, Freight, Items, Main Category)
[order_payments] ──────────────────────────► Order Payments Aggregated (Payment Value, Max Installments, Type)
[order_reviews] ───────────────────────────► Order Reviews Aggregated (Average Review Score)
                                                      │
                                                      ▼
[olist_orders_dataset] + [customers] ────────► Master Orders DataFrame (112k+ transactions)
                                                      │
                                                      ▼
GroupBy `customer_unique_id` ────────────────► Master Customer Feature Matrix (96k+ Unique Buyers)
```

## 4. Feature Engineering: RFM + Behavioral Extensions

To capture the holistic customer profile beyond standard RFM, we engineered 7 core dimensions:

1. **Recency ($R$):** Days elapsed since the customer's last purchase relative to the dataset snapshot date.
2. **Frequency ($F$):** Total number of completed orders placed by the customer.
3. **Monetary ($M$):** Lifetime revenue generated by the customer.
4. **Average Order Value ($AOV$):** Monetary divided by Frequency.
5. **Satisfaction Score ($CSAT$):** Mean star rating (1–5) left across post-delivery surveys.
6. **Logistics Experience ($Delay$):** Mean delivery delay in days ($Date_{delivered} - Date_{estimated}$).
7. **Financial Behavior ($Installments$):** Mean credit card installments utilized.

---

## 5. Machine Learning Clustering & Evaluation

### 5.1 Preprocessing & Scaling
- **Log Transformation ($\log(1+x)$):** Applied to highly right-skewed features ($R, F, M, AOV$) to normalize feature distributions.
- **Robust Scaling:** RobustScaler applied to mitigate the effect of extreme monetary/frequency outliers.

### 5.2 Model Hyperparameter Selection ($K=4$)
We evaluated $K$-Means clustering across $K \in [2, 8]$ using three complementary mathematical criteria:

- **Elbow Method (WCSS / Inertia):** Observed clear inflection ("elbow") at $K=4$.
- **Silhouette Score:** Reached optimal peak at $K=4$ ($\text{Silhouette Score} = 0.482$), indicating high intra-cluster tightness and distinct inter-cluster separation.
- **Davies-Bouldin Index:** Minimized at $K=4$ ($\text{DB Index} = 0.81$).

### Segment Detailed Breakdown:

1. **👑 Cluster 0: Champions & VIP Shoppers**
   - **Characteristics:** Lowest recency, highest repeat purchase frequency, highest lifetime spend, excellent review satisfaction.
   - **Financial Behavior:** Frequently use credit card with low installments. High brand trust.

2. **💳 Cluster 1: High-Value Big Spenders**
   - **Characteristics:** High single-order ticket value, heavy reliance on maximum credit card installments (6–10x).
   - **Product Preference:** Electronics, furniture, computer accessories, premium appliances.

3. **⚠️ Cluster 2: At-Risk & Logistics Dissatisfied**
   - **Characteristics:** Abnormally low review scores (average rating 1.2–2.0), severe delivery delay (gaps up to 15+ days past estimated date).
   - **Root Cause:** Operational failure in partner logistics network, not product defect.

4. **🛒 Cluster 3: Core Occasional Buyers**
   - **Characteristics:** Single purchase, standard category item, smooth delivery, high review score (4.7+).
   - **Opportunity:** High potential for cross-selling and conversion to repeat buyers.

## 7. Actionable Business Strategy & Action Matrix

### 📢 7.1 Marketing Team: Ad Targeting & Campaign Playbook

1. **For Champions / VIPs:**
   - **Strategy:** VIP Loyalty Program & Exclusive Pre-Access.
   - **Ads Strategy:** Retargeting with zero-discount premium ad creatives, early access to new product launches.
   - **Channel:** Direct Email, WhatsApp VIP Club, Push Notifications.

2. **For High-Value Big Spenders:**
   - **Strategy:** Installment Incentive & High-Ticket Cross-Sell.
   - **Ads Strategy:** Highlight "0% Interest Installments" (Sem Juros) on premium categories (Tech/Furniture).
   - **Channel:** Instagram/Facebook Carousel Ads featuring complementary accessories.

3. **For At-Risk / Logistics Dissatisfied:**
   - **Strategy:** Service Recovery & Apology Campaigns.
   - **Ads Strategy:** Suppress standard promotional sales ads immediately! Send automated apology vouchers ($15–$20 credit) with guaranteed express delivery.
   - **Channel:** Dedicated Customer Care Email & SMS.

4. **For Core Occasional Buyers:**
   - **Strategy:** First Repeat Purchase Activation.
   - **Ads Strategy:** Category-affinity retargeting based on their first purchased category within 30 days of purchase.
   - **Channel:** Dynamic Facebook Product Ads (DPA).

---

### 💻 7.2 Product & Dev Team: Recommendation System Integration

1. **Segment-Aware Collaborative Filtering:**
   - Inject `cluster_id` as a context feature vector into the Recommendation Model (Matrix Factorization / DeepFM).
   - Show high-ticket installment items to *Cluster 1*, and high-rated trending items to *Cluster 3*.

2. **Real-time UX Personalization:**
   - **VIP Badge & Concierge Support:** Display VIP status badge on user profile for Cluster 0.
   - **Logistics Alert Banner:** For Cluster 2 returning users, display proactive delivery status tracking and dedicated support widgets.
